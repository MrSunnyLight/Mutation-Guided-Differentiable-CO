This repo is for our paper: 

## **Mutation-Guided Differentiable Quadratic Combinatorial Optimization** 

**Authors**: Yongliang Sun, Ismail Alkhouri, Cheng-Han Huang, Alvaro Velasquez, Susmit Jha, Rongrong Wang

- The paper can be found in https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06921

- This paper provides CPU solvers for MIS and MaxCut (mqo-mis, and mqo-maxcut), and GPU solvers for MIS and MaxCut (pmqo-mis, and pmqo-maxcut).

- To evaluate graphs that are not conssidered in the paper, it is recommended to tune the step size, momentum, and reset parameter.
